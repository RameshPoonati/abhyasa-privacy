Privacy Policy — Abhyasa Kerala

Application: com.abhyasam.kerala · Effective date: 30 September 2026

Abhyasa Kerala is an independent exam-preparation app for Kerala PSC previous-year question papers. Questions and your practice progress are available offline.

Data collection

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics. We do not operate a server that receives your practice progress.

Internet access and external services

Question content ships with the app and works offline. If you open an official source, app update, or sharing link, Android opens another app or website. That service may process your request under its own privacy policy. Google Play also processes information needed to distribute and update the app. See Google's privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Your reading position, language, and display preferences are stored in private app storage on your device. Android backup and restore may retain app data according to your device settings. You can clear local data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, or microphone access. It may request notification permission for optional study reminders.

Children

The app is a study aid intended for adult exam aspirants and is not directed at children.

Content sources

The collection includes previous papers and source-labelled memory-based or practice sets. Abhyasa Kerala is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Kerala PSC or any government body. Verify recruitment information with the relevant official source.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated before the corresponding app update is released.
