Privacy Policy — Abhyasa State Exams

Effective: 10 September 2026 · Application: com.abhyasam.states

Abhyasa State Exams is an independent exam-practice app. There is no account registration, advertising or analytics service in the app.

Information on your device

The app stores reading position, selected content, language and text-size preferences in private app storage. We do not operate a server that receives your practice progress. You can clear local data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Android device backup and restore may retain app data according to your device settings.

Downloads and external services

When you request a state or exam pack, Google Play downloads its question content to your device. Google Play and its delivery services process the information needed to distribute and update apps under Google's privacy policy. Downloaded packs are prepared in private app storage for offline use. An internet connection is needed for downloads and updates.

Opening an official source link takes you to an external website, which processes requests under its own policy. Google Play and external sites may process network and device information to provide their services. See Google's privacy policy.

Support messages

If you choose to email support, we receive the email address and information you send. We use these messages to respond and investigate the reported issue. Do not include sensitive personal information. Request deletion of a support conversation by emailing the same address.

Permissions and audience

The app does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access. Content is stored in its private app directory. The app is intended for adult exam aspirants and is not directed at children.

Independent study material

Exam names identify study material. This app does not represent or have an affiliation with any government body or exam authority. Verify recruitment information with the relevant official source.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when app practices change. Contact: youlicit@gmail.com.
